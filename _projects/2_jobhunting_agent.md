> This project is still under active development.

An AI-powered multi-agent system that combines study assistance with job hunting. Users upload their course materials — slides, PDFs, notes — and the system builds a RAG knowledge base from them. A **Planner Agent** creates a study schedule based on exam dates and progress; a **Tutor Agent** generates quizzes and adapts difficulty dynamically; an **Evaluator Agent** tracks recurring knowledge gaps rather than just individual mistakes.

In parallel, a **Career Agent** continuously analyzes the user's CV, completed courses, and demonstrated skills to search for internships and entry-level positions on platforms like LinkedIn, StepStone, and Indeed. Each job is scored against the user's profile via semantic matching. The core idea: the better you learn, the better matches the system surfaces.
